# Operative Report

*Demo document, fictional.*

**Procedure:** Right knee arthroscopy, ACL reconstruction with bone-patellar tendon-bone autograft, repair of posterior horn medial meniscus.

**Surgeon:** Dr. Marcus Oyelaran  
**Anesthesia:** General with adductor canal block

## Findings

Complete mid-substance ACL rupture. 8 mm vertical tear, posterior horn medial meniscus, red-white zone. Articular cartilage Outerbridge grade 0–1 throughout.

## Procedure

1. Diagnostic arthroscopy confirmed findings.
2. Meniscal repair with two all-inside sutures.
3. Central-third patellar tendon graft harvested (10 mm).
4. Femoral and tibial tunnels drilled; graft fixed with interference screws. Graft taut through full range with no impingement.

**Complications:** None. **EBL:** Minimal.

## Post-op orders

Brace locked in extension for ambulation 2 weeks. Toe-touch weight bearing 4 weeks (meniscal repair). PT to begin at 2 weeks.
